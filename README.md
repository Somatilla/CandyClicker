# Features:
Big candy that u can click all you want
## Buildings
if you have 20 candies you can get a: Clicker (increases candies you make every click)<br>
if you have 100 candies you can get a: CandyTree (You get a 0.03 increase in your candy-per-second)<br>
if you have 300 candies you can get a: Factory (You get a 0.1 increase in your candy-per-second)<br>
if you have 500 candies you can get a: CandyCity (You get a 0.2 increase in your candy-per-second)<br>
if you have 1000 candies you can get a: Candy planet (You get a 0.35 increase in your candy-per-second)<br>
if you have 10000 candies you can get a: Candy Galaxy (You get a 1 increase in your candy-per-second)<br>
if you have 15000 candies you can get one: DOGE coin (You get a 2.3 increase in your candy-per-second)<br>

<br>
## Upgrades
if you go to the upgrades you can purchase:<br>
  - hardworking mouse (500c) doubles your clicker production<br>
  - golden fruits (999c) doubles your CandyTree production<br>
  - overtime (2000c) doubles your factory production <br>
<br>
## Random candies!
If you are playing for a while, you may notice colorful candies appearing randomly on your screen!<br>
They do different things depending on their color!:<br>
  - The blue one gives you 200 candies!<br>
  - The Yellow one triggers a candy rain!<br>
  - The Green one boosts production for a bit!<br>
  - the purple one TAKES 300 candies! (i suggest waiting for it to despawn)<br>
