<br>
# Features:
Big candy that u can click all you want
if you have 20 candies you can get a: Clicker (increases candies you make every click)<br>
if you have 100 candies you can get a: CandyTree (You get a 1.03 increase in your candy-per-second)<br>
if you have 300 candies you can get a: Factory (You get a 1.1 increase in your candy-per-second)<br>
if you have 600 candies you can get a: CandyCity (You get a 1.2 increase in your candy-per-second)<br>
<br>
if you go to the upgrades you can purchase:<br>
  - hardworking mouse (500c) doubles your clicker production<br>
  - golden fruits (999c) doubles your CandyTree production<br>
  - overtime (2000c) doubles your factory production <br>
